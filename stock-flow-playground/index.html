Expression & unit rules

Use stable IDs, not names: k * upper. Arithmetic: + − * /, unary signs, parentheses, min(a,b), max(a,b), abs(a). Decimal/scientific numbers. Fixed information lag: delay(upper, 2) reads upper’s amount two fixed dt ticks earlier. Steps must be a literal integer 0–100; only stock IDs, not arbitrary expressions, auxiliaries or nested delays. Negative ticks hold initial stock; zero reads old state. A delay-capable model requires horizon/dt to be an integer: no shorter terminal step. No time variable, code, property access, or references to flows. Auxiliary cycles fail. Max 512 characters and AST depth 24.

Stock amounts must be nonnegative; negative flow rates fail. Endpoint stock unit labels must match; flow units must be amount/time. Auxiliary/parameter units are labels only. Expression dimensions are not checked.

Information delay is not material transit
