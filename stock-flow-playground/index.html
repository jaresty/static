Amounts, rates, and information
Stocks hold nonnegative amounts. Flows request amounts per time from the old state. Simultaneous outflows share available source stock proportionally. Incoming material becomes available for outflow next step. Inspect requested/applied rates, source factors and cumulative accounting in the inspector.
Information delay is not material transit
0.2 * delay(upper, 2) requests a transfer now using an observation two fixed dt ticks earlier. The current source still limits debit and receipt immediately. Negative ticks hold initial stock; zero lag reads old state. Only stock IDs and literal integer delays 0–100 are permitted. Delay-capable models require integer horizon/dt.
To represent water in a pipe, create a third stock Water in transit, a flow into it and a flow out. Transit accumulates actual material; delaying a rate does not.
Expression & unit rules
Use stable IDs, not names: k * upper. Decimal/scientific numbers; arithmetic + − * /; unary signs; parentheses; min(a,b), max(a,b), abs(a). No time variable, code, properties, flow references, delayed arbitrary expressions or auxiliary cycles. Max 512 characters and AST depth 24.
Endpoint stock units must match exactly. Flow units are amount/time. Parameter/auxiliary units are descriptive labels; expression dimensions are not checked. Negative rates fail; reverse transfers need a separate flow.
Limits and interpretation
Hypothetical equations, not prediction or empirical validation. Explicit Euler is timestep-sensitive; compare smaller dt. Source limiting is an accounting policy, not a physical realism guarantee. Max 40 entities / 2,000 transitions. No baseline or abstract 0–100 levels.
Keyboard & local storage
Use the object list, endpoint forms and View X/Y for keyboard editing. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Shift+Z redoes. Space toggles playback outside forms/dialogs. Autosave is not a backup; Download for durable copies. Invalid saved data is not overwritten without explicit replacement. No accounts, provider calls or telemetry.
