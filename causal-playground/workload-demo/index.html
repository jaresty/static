04 / Read the assumptions

A small model, not a law of work

Two states; two flows

B is unfinished homogeneous, divisible work. S is a hypothetical dimensionless stress index. Arrivals A, completions C and nominal capacity K are in work/day. Workload pressure W and productivity P are auxiliaries, not extra states.

W = B / (K × Tw)
target S = W / (1 + W)
dS/dt = (target S − S) / τ
P = 1 − α × S
C = min(K × P, B / Tc)
dB/dt = A − C

Tc = 1 day · Tw = 2 days · τ = 2 days
α = 0.5 (on) or 0 (off); default K = 10

Initial backlog is a one-time shock, never counted as an arrival. A sustained arrival rate adds work every day. Compare the two presets: 20 initial work with A=4 versus no initial work with A=12. This is a comparison of inputs to these formulas, not a curve contest with the existing engine.

Why recovery does not mean zero

With continuing A=4, K=10 and α=.5, B=4 gives W=.2, S=1/6 and P=11/12. Effective capacity is 55/6 work/day, so C=min(55/6,4)=4. Arrivals balance completions at 4 unfinished work units. This follows from the chosen B/Tc service law; it is not observed workplace behavior.

Numerical method & precision

Explicit Euler uses old-state rates: Bnext=B+h(A−C), Snext=S+h(target−S)/τ. Internal h=1/4096 day; comparison h/2=1/8192 day. Display samples are 0.25 day apart. Changes are right-continuous and aligned using integer step indices. A change at day 20 affects the interval starting at 20, not the interval ending there.

For this template, h≤min(Tc,τ) preserves nonnegative backlog and a convex stress update; this is not a universal accuracy guarantee. There is no upper backlog cap, clipping or correction that discards work. Display rounding is not used in the ledger. A finite h gives an approximation; very small backlog is not a promise of integer-job completion.

Inputs are bounded; horizon is fixed at 120 days. Each run computes 1,474,560 internal steps across two grids and retains 481 samples per grid. The indicator reports maximum common-sample differences against predeclared thresholds (.1 work, .002 stress). A failure is disclosed, not retuned. Selected exact-case and refinement tests support this bounded choice, not every possible input or unsampled instant.

No heterogeneity, deadlines, staffing, learning, uncertainty, calibration or predictive validity. Stress impairment is an illustrative hypothesis. Numerical agreement does not validate domain assumptions.
